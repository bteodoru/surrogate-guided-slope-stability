Learning curve on test-natural, RF, for B (seed + Latin hypercube extension) and C (seed + the linear-bin guided design); C-log, C-seq, C-logG, the pool arms and every C$_0$ ablation are not covered. Hyper-parameters are fixed at every budget, not tuned per point: scikit-learn defaults. Budgets 100 and 200 are random subsets of the 300-point seed, shared by both curves; 300 is the seed itself; 400 and 500 add a random subset of the arm's 300 added points to the seed, so the subset of C is not itself a guided design at that budget; 600 is the arm's whole training set. Points are means over 10 subsamples, and the band is plus or minus one standard deviation over them; at 300 and 600 there is a single fit and no band. The band covers subsampling only. Panels: global RMSE (FOS), RMSE on the selection range (FOS), global RMSE of log(FOS), and RMSE (FOS) in the highest reporting zone. The curve is not used to extrapolate the relocation to other budgets.
