Supplementary ablation. C0-log and C0-seq train on the guided points alone, without the seed. Training-set sizes: B 600, C-log 600, C-seq 600, C0-log 300, C0-seq 300. Red: C0 minus B, 95\% Welch interval. Purple: C minus the corresponding C0, 95\% paired interval over the realizations, because within a realization C0 trains on exactly the guided points that C adds to the seed. The two differences are oriented oppositely; in both, a negative value means that the first-named arm has the smaller log-RMSE. Every comparison joins arms trained on different budgets and is not a like-for-like comparison. Means over the five confirmatory realizations, on the half-open reporting zones; grey shading marks the two bins outside the selection range, and hollow markers the bins with fewer than 10 test points. The intervals visualize variability between designs and constitute no test; an interval containing 0 is inconclusive, not a tie.
