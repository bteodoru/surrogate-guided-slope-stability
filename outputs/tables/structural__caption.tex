Per-bin training density and per-bin error, both as a difference against B, over the five confirmatory realizations. Every interval, on the density panel and on the error panels alike, visualizes variability between designs and constitutes no test; the fifteen intervals are a diagnostic, not fifteen tests. The pattern is consistent with density-driven relocation; it is not evidence that density caused it. The small numbers above the error panels are the test points per bin. Hollow markers, on the error panels, are bins with fewer than 10 of them; they are shown but carry no statement in the text. Both the densities and the errors in this figure are read from the replication metrics and use the half-open reporting zones; the allocation and displacement tables use the generator's binning instead, which differs only on an exact bin edge.
