Per-bin training density (raw counts; B, C-log, C-seq, C-logG, C-oracle and C-logP all train on 600 points) and per-bin log-RMSE, each as the difference between an arm's mean over the five confirmatory realizations and B's, with 95\% Welch intervals. A negative error difference means a smaller log-RMSE than B. Every interval, on the density panel and on the error panels alike, visualizes variability between designs and constitutes no test; read together they are a per-bin diagnostic, not a set of tests. The pattern is consistent with density-driven relocation; it is not evidence that density caused it. The small numbers above the upper error panels are the test points per bin. Hollow markers, on the error panels, are bins with fewer than 10 of them; they are shown but carry no statement in the text. Both the densities and the errors in this figure are read from the replication metrics and use the half-open reporting zones; the allocation and displacement tables use the generator's binning instead, which differs only on an exact bin edge. C-oracle, C-logP and their C$_0$ ablations exist only in r1-r5; they have no r0.
