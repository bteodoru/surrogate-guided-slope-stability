Screened points are selected among 200\,000 candidates by the screener's predicted FOS, in 15 bins on the selection range [0.5, 6.0]; the screener is a random forest trained on the seed (C-seq: refitted on the seed and its earlier batches; C-logG: gradient boosting). The pool arms C-oracle and C-logP draw C-log's allocation from one sub-pool of the solved reference pool per realization: their points were solved before they were selected, so their count (†) is not a budget result. They are a diagnostic reference, not a sampling strategy. C$_0$, C$_0$-log, C$_0$-seq, C$_0$-logG, C$_0$-oracle and C$_0$-logP are ablations: they train on the added points without the seed, carry no budget and are not like-for-like comparisons with the budget arms. r0 is the existing sets and is exploratory; r1–r5 are new realizations of the added points on the fixed seed, so A, the seed alone, has none.}
