Primary criterion: log-RMSE against B on the decision range, FOS in [0.5, 2), and on its two halves, [0.5, 1) and [1, 2). Each point is the mean over the confirmatory realizations r1-r5 of the arm's log-RMSE minus B's, with its 95\% Welch interval; a negative difference means a smaller log-RMSE than B. Each row carries the four learner-by-test cells: circles RF, squares GBM; filled markers test-natural, hollow markers test-stratified. The rows share one x axis within each column; the columns have their own. The intervals cover variability between designs, conditional on the fixed seed, the fixed test sets and the common hyper-parameters fixed from the seed; they constitute no test. An interval containing 0 is inconclusive, never a tie or evidence of equivalence. C-seq and C-logG are sensitivity arms, reported in Supplementary. The C$_0$ rows are ablations: they train on the 300 guided points without the seed, against the 600 of B, so they join different budgets and are not a like-for-like comparison. A is absent: it has no replications, only its existing set (r0), and therefore no interval; C and the original C$_0$ are absent for the same reason, as they were never replicated. r0 is exploratory and enters no interval, so it is not drawn; C-oracle, C-logP and their C$_0$ ablations have no r0. Bins are half-open, [lo, hi), as everywhere the replication metrics are read.
