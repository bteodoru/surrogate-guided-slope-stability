Predicted bin (rows) against true bin (columns) for the points the screener selected, C-log and C-seq, pooled over the confirmatory realizations r1-r5; r0 is excluded. Each row is normalized by the number of points selected into that predicted bin, given beside its label; a predicted bin into which nothing was selected is shown grey. Bins follow the generator, (lo, hi], so the outer columns are FOS < 0.5 and FOS > 6. The matrices describe the screener only on the points it selected, which were predicted inside the selection range and inside a deficit bin; they are not its accuracy over the candidate pool. Off-diagonal mass includes compensating errors, which leave achieved minus intended untouched. None of this says what a perfect implementation would do; that is the oracle's question.
